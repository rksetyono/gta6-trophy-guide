    ⚠ The one trophy you can miss

    Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida. During Chapter 1, one morning at Jason’s house after the mission Down to Earth, Lucia talks about meditating in the kitchen and walks out to the balcony. Follow her and accept the optional quest. That balcony meditation is gone after Chapter 1, and it is not yet confirmed whether the trophy needs it — do it while you can. Every other trophy can be earned after the story in free roam.

    Source: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap. See the trophy’s own guide below for the full detail.

    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough, about 200 hours, no online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no mission medals. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter — there is no mission replay — and keep cheats off: they disable trophies and saving.
